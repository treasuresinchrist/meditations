---
title: "Five Unchangeable Portions"
---

We live in a world of constant change. Governments rise and fall. Wealth disappears. Friends leave us. Strength fades. The things around us are uncertain and temporary.

Yet the believer's confidence does not rest upon changing circumstances, but upon the unchanging God.

Scripture presents many things that never change. Here are five precious certainties that anchor the believer's heart.

---

## 1. An Unchangeable God

> **"For I am the LORD, I change not; therefore ye sons of Jacob are not consumed."**  
> **Malachi 3:6**

Our security rests not upon our faithfulness, but upon God's unchanging character.

It is striking that the Lord says **"ye sons of Jacob."** Jacob reminds us of weakness, failure, and inconsistency. Yet God's covenant stands because **He never changes**.

In days of uncertainty, we look away from ourselves to the One who declares:

> **"I am the LORD, I change not."**

Because He is unchanging, His promises remain sure.

---

## 2. An Unchangeable Saviour

> **"Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever."**  
> **Hebrews 13:8**

The Lord Jesus Christ never changes.

- **Yesterday** — He bore our sins upon the cross (1 Peter 2:24).
- **Today** — He intercedes for us as our Great High Priest (Hebrews 7:25).
- **Forever** — We shall be with Him for all eternity (1 Thessalonians 4:17).

His work is complete.
His love is unchanged.
His Person is eternal.

The Saviour who saved us is the same Saviour who sustains us today and will soon receive us to Himself forever.

---

## 3. An Unchangeable Comforter

> **"And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever."**  
> **John 14:16**

When the Lord Jesus returned to the Father, He did not leave His people as orphans.

He sent **another Comforter**—the Holy Spirit.

The Spirit teaches, comforts, strengthens, guides, and intercedes for every believer.

Though we may grieve Him or quench His working, He never abandons those whom He has sealed.

He abides with us **forever**.

---

## 4. An Unchangeable Word

> **"The word of the Lord endureth forever."**  
> **1 Peter 1:25**

Everything around us changes.

Opinions change.
Cultures change.
Books are forgotten.

But God's Word remains.

It is the inspired Word that gives life, nourishes the believer, cleanses the heart, establishes the soul, and equips us for every good work.

Many have tried to overthrow it, yet generation after generation has passed while the Scriptures continue unchanged.

The Word that stood in Peter's day still stands today.

---

## 5. An Unchangeable Inheritance

> **"...to an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away, reserved in heaven for you."**  
> **1 Peter 1:4**

Everything on earth eventually fades.

Our heavenly inheritance does not.

It is:

- **Incorruptible** — untouched by decay.
- **Undefiled** — untouched by sin.
- **Unfading** — untouched by time.

The inheritance prepared by God is eternal because it is kept by an eternal God.

---

## Our Unchanging Hope

The world changes.

But:

- God remains.
- Christ remains.
- The Holy Spirit remains.
- God's Word remains.
- Our inheritance remains.


> **"They shall perish; but thou remainest... Thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail."**  
> **Hebrews 1:11–12**

When everything around us seems uncertain, our hearts can rest upon Him who never changes.
