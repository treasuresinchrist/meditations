---
title: "Three Sweet Smells"
---
Scripture presents three beautiful fragrances that rise before God: **the sacrifice of Christ, the life of the Christian, and the giving of the believer.**

## 1. The Sweet Smell of Christ's Death

> **And walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us, and hath given Himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweetsmelling savour.**  
> --Ephesians 5:2

The death of Christ was the perfect sacrifice, offered in love and wholly acceptable to God. Nothing can compare with the fragrance of His finished work.

Christ did not merely die for us; He **gave Himself**. His sacrifice is the foundation of every blessing we have received.

---

## 2. The Sweet Smell of Christian Life

> **For we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ, in them that are saved, and in them that perish.**  
> --2 Corinthians 2:15

The believer's life is meant to carry the fragrance of Christ.

Our testimony is not merely about what we say, but about what is seen of Christ in our lives. As we walk in fellowship with Him, something of His character should become evident through us.

The fragrance is **"of Christ."** We do not produce the fragrance ourselves; it is Christ expressed through the believer.

---

## 3. The Sweet Smell of Christian Giving

> **But I have all, and abound: I am full, having received of Epaphroditus the things which were sent from you, an odour of a sweet smell, a sacrifice acceptable, wellpleasing to God.**<br>
> --Philippians 4:18

Paul regarded the Philippians' gift not merely as material support, but as a **sacrifice offered to God**.

Christian giving becomes worship when it flows from love for Christ and concern for His servants and work.

God sees not only what is given, but the heart and sacrifice behind the giving.

---
| Fragrance | What it speaks of                    |
|---|--------------------------------------|
| **Christ's death** | Christ's sacrifice                   |
| **Christian life** | Christ expressed through us          |
| **Christian giving** | Our sacrifice offered to God for Him |

The order is beautiful:
**Christ's sacrifice → Christ's life in us → our sacrifice for Him.**

The first is the foundation.  
The second is the fragrance of Christ seen in our walk.  
The third is the fragrance of our response in practical sacrifice.

May the fragrance of Christ be seen in our lives and rise before God in our lives.
