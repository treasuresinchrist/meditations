---
title: "God's Perfect Attributes"
---

Scripture repeatedly reminds us that there is none like our God.

## 1. The Only Lord God

> **"...the only Lord God..."**  
> **Jude 4**

God alone possesses absolute authority and rightful lordship. Every other authority is derived and temporary; His is eternal and supreme.

---

## 2. The Only True God

> **"And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent."**  
> -- John 17:3

There are many gods worshipped by men, but only one is true. Eternal life is found in knowing the Father through His Son.

---

## 3. The Only Wise God

> **"Now unto the King eternal, immortal, invisible, the only wise God, be honour and glory for ever and ever. Amen."**  
> -- 1 Timothy 1:17

God's wisdom is perfect, infinite, and beyond all human understanding. We may not always understand His ways, but we can always trust His wisdom.

---

## 4. The Only Immortal God

> **"...who only hath immortality, dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto..."**  
> -- 1 Timothy 6:16

God alone possesses immortality inherently. All created life depends upon Him, but His existence is eternal, independent, and unchanging.

---

## 5. The Only Powerful God

> **"...the blessed and only Potentate, the King of kings, and Lord of lords."**  
> -- 1 Timothy 6:15

Every earthly ruler receives authority temporarily. God alone possesses absolute and sovereign power. His throne cannot be shaken, and His purposes cannot fail.

---

## 6. The Only Holy God

> **"Who shall not fear thee, O Lord, and glorify thy name? for thou only art holy..."**  
> -- Revelation 15:4

Holiness belongs to God in its fullest sense. He is completely separate from sin, perfectly pure, and the standard by which all holiness is measured.

---

## 7. The Only Forgiving God

> **"Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but God only?"**  
> -- Mark 2:7

The scribes spoke more truly than they realised. Since sin is ultimately against God, only God can forgive it. Christ's authority to forgive sins reveals His deity.

---

These seven "only" statements remind us that God is utterly unique.

- The only **Lord** God.
- The only **True** God.
- The only **Wise** God.
- The only **Immortal** God.
- The only **Powerful** God.
- The only **Holy** God.
- The only **Forgiving** God.

No one can be compared with Him.

> **"To whom then will ye liken God? or what likeness will ye compare unto Him?"**  
> -- Isaiah 40:18
